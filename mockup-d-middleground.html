What if I want a D minor scale but with a dark feel?
Dark over D minor → two flavors: D harmonic minor (that raised 7th = tension, almost flamenco) or D phrygian (flat 2 = pure menace). I've swapped the last two boards to show both — the phrygian E♭ is your "dark" note. Lean on it.
🤖 AI COACH · ask anything about the song you're playing
What key is this and why does the Fm feel so dark?
You're in F major, but that Fm is a borrowed iv chord — taking the minor four from F minor. It's the classic "sunshine turns to shadow" move. Try letting it ring longer than written.
Give me an easier voicing for E♭ at capo 3.
With capo 3, E♭ = a C shape. Use C-add9 (x32033) — two fingers move from your G shape, much smoother in this progression.

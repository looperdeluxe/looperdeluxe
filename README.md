## **🌌 Core Features: Powering Your Practice 🎸**

### 1. The Multi-Media Pipeline

*   **What it is:** A unified drag-and-drop loader for all your audio and video files. It's truly effortless.
*   **How it works (Surface Level):** Drop any track or video file directly in. The app instantly builds a crisp [WaveSurfer.js](https://wavesurfer-js.org/) audio timeline or fires up a fully scalable video deck. Go fullscreen with a click, or mirror video footage for visual transposition – an absolute lifesaver for mirroring a guitarist's hands from your perspective.

### 2. The Fuzzy Lyric Sync Engine

*   **What it is:** A robust lyric lookup that handles messy file names without breaking a sweat.
*   **How it works (Surface Level):** Type in a track name. The engine uses regex to scrub out common metadata like "(Official Video)" or "[Remastered]" that typically interfere with database searches. It then queries [LRCLIB](https://www.www.lrclib.net/). If exact timestamps are available, you get perfectly synced karaoke lyrics. If not, it intelligently distributes plain text lyrics across the timeline, so your screen is never empty, and you're always connected to the song.

### 3. Smart CAGED Chord Sequencer & Matrix

*   **What it is:** A progression tracker that remembers *your* specific, preferred chord shapes. No more generic diagrams.
*   **How it works (Surface Level):** Input a progression like "G D Em C." Each "chord pill" on your timeline gains its own custom HTML data memory. This allows you to set your G to a C-shape major, your D to an A-shape minor, or any voicing you prefer. As the engine advances through the song, it dynamically updates the fretboard canvas to display your exact customized voicings. It's personalized learning built right in.

### 4. Self-Recording & Playback

*   **What it is:** Record yourself playing along, directly within the app.
*   **How it works (Surface Level):** Hit record, play your part, and the app captures your audio. This recording is then integrated into the session, allowing for immediate review and comparison against the original track. It's a powerful tool for self-assessment and tracking your progress.

### 5. Quick Studio Utilities

*   **The cool extra stuff:** Utilize active A/B looping banks to isolate and master challenging sections, leverage lightning-fast hotkeys ('I' for in, 'O' for out, 'L' for loop, and Spacebar for play/pause), and stay in time with a standalone metronome. All the details that enhance your workflow.

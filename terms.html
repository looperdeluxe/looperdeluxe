Terms of Use

Looper Deluxe is a personal practice tool for musicians. By using it you agree to the following.

What Looper Deluxe does — and doesn't — do with music

Looper Deluxe helps you practice along with music you already have lawful access to: songs you stream through your own YouTube or Spotify account, or audio files you own. It does not sell, host, or distribute recordings.

  • Chord and beat data are facts. Our chord library stores chord names, timings, tempo, and song structure — factual, non-copyrightable analysis data. It never stores recordings or full lyrics.
  • Audio analysis is ephemeral. When a feature analyzes audio (tab capture, microphone, or a file you provide), processing happens in your browser or transiently in memory. We do not retain copies of copyrighted recordings.
  • Stem isolation runs on your device. The instrument isolator processes audio locally in your browser for practice purposes. Nothing is uploaded or stored.
  • Lyrics are displayed via third-party lyric services for synchronized practice display.

Your responsibilities

Use Looper Deluxe only with content you have lawful access to, for personal, non-commercial practice. Don't record, export, or redistribute copyrighted recordings or stems. You're responsible for complying with the terms of the streaming services you connect (YouTube, Spotify) and with applicable copyright law in your country.

Community contributions

Chord corrections and community versions you submit are factual annotations you license to us to display to other users. Don't submit content copied from other tab/chord services.

Accounts & data

Streaming connections (e.g., Spotify) use your own account via official APIs; tokens stay in your browser. We store practice data (saved loops, setlists, preferences) locally in your browser.

Looper Deluxe is provided as-is, without warranties. Chord detection is automated and may be imperfect — flagged/unverified charts are marked as such.
